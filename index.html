The problem

Flat photos don't sell land

Lot listings live or die on trust and clarity — two things a handful of blurry photos rarely deliver.

✕

Buyers can't picture the lot

A few static photos don't show orientation, surroundings, or what it actually feels like standing on the property.

✕

Sold vs. available is unclear

Partially-sold lots confuse buyers — nobody can tell which portion is actually still on the market.

✕

Out-of-town buyers can't visit

OFWs and overseas buyers make up a huge share of land purchases in the Philippines, and flying home just to look isn't realistic.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to install anything to view a tour?

No. Every Land360 tour runs directly in the browser — on desktop or mobile — with no app to download.

What if only part of my lot is sold?

The Standard and Premium plans include a sold/available boundary overlay drawn directly onto the 360° tour, so buyers immediately see what's still on the market.

Can buyers submit inquiries directly from the tour?

Yes — every plan includes a "Request Viewing" form that captures name, email, phone, and message straight to you.

How long does setup take?

Most listings go live within 3–7 days of the on-site capture, depending on location and plan.

What happens after my hosting period ends?

You can renew hosting at a reduced rate, or download your assets. We'll notify you before your listing expires.
